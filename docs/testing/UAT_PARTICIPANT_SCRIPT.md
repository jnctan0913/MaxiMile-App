## Welcome

Hi, and thank you so much for taking the time to help us today.

We're testing an app called **MaxiMile**, a credit card miles optimizer for Singapore. The goal of today's session is to see how easy the app is to understand and use. We want to hear your honest reactions — there are **no right or wrong answers**.

A few important things before we start:

- **You are testing the app. The app is not testing you.** If something is confusing, that's valuable feedback for us to improve.
- **Please think out loud as you go.** Tell us what you're looking for, what you expect to happen, and anything that surprises or confuses you. The more you say, the more helpful it is.
- **I won't answer "how do I do this?" questions** during the tasks — we want to see how you naturally navigate the app. But I may ask you questions about what you're thinking.

---

## How This Session Works

We'll go through **6 short scenarios** together. For each one, I'll read you a task and ask you to use the app to complete it.

**Remember**:
- Talk out loud as you go
- There is no time pressure — take your time
- If you feel stuck, just tell me what you're thinking
- You can ask me to repeat a task at any time

---

*Please say "done" when you've found your answer.*
